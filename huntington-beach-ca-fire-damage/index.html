Asbestos and Lead Paint Hazards

Homes built before the 1980s in Huntington Beach, CA often contain asbestos in insulation, roof materials, and floor tiles that fire damage may disturb. Attempting to remove or work around asbestos without professional containment and procedures causes serious health damage. Inhaling asbestos fibers leads to mesothelioma, lung cancer, and asbestosis that often develop decades after exposure. Lead paint in older homes becomes dangerous when fire disturbs painted surfaces and creates lead dust. Both asbestos and lead require licensed professionals and certified procedures for safe handling in Huntington Beach, CA. DIY handling of these materials violates environmental regulations and creates severe health hazards. Professional abatement contractors in Huntington Beach know how to safely contain and remove these hazardous materials during fire restoration.
