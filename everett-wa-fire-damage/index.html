When complete duct replacement is not feasible or affordable, some Everett restoration teams use sealed encapsulation to prevent odor from migrating from contaminated ducts into living spaces. Encapsulation is less expensive than replacement and can extend the life of questionable ductwork, but it does not eliminate the odor itself.
