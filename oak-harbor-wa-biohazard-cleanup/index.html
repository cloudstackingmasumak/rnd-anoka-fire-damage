Inspecting for Structural and Hidden Damage

Once the main cleanup is complete, walk through your Oak Harbor home carefully to check for any overlooked damage. Look for discoloration on walls, ceilings, or under furniture. Check baseboards and corners where moisture or residue may have spread. Open closets and cabinets, and inspect shelves. Pay attention to areas where blood or bodily fluids may have seeped into drywall, flooring, or insulation.
