Year-Round Fire Prevention

Regardless of season, your electrical system needs regular attention. Check outlets for signs of overheating, discoloration, or burns. Feel electrical outlets and switches for warmth. If an outlet is warm, stop using it immediately and call an electrician. Verify that circuit breakers trip properly. Never use a breaker that won't reset. Have it inspected and replaced. Check appliances for damaged cords or signs of damage. Unplug any that seem unsafe and have them serviced. Space heaters, air conditioning units, and other high-draw appliances need proper circuits and shouldn't be plugged into extension cords or power strips. Review your home's electrical load annually. Modern homes add substantial electrical demand over the years. Your original electrical panel may be inadequate for current usage. An electrician can evaluate and upgrade if needed. These checks only take a few minutes but catch dangerous problems before they start fires.
