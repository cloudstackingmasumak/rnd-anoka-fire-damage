Attic and Soffit Damage

Attic insulation in a fire-damaged Punta Gorda home almost always requires complete removal and replacement. Insulation acts like a sponge, holding soot and smoke odor indefinitely.

Structural wood framing absorbs water readily and dries slowly, especially when surrounded by insulation that slows evaporation. Moisture meters measure water content by inserting pins into suspect wood members, providing numerical readings that indicate whether wood is wet, damp, or dry. Different wood species and thicknesses require different drying times. A 2-by-6 framing member might dry in 30 days under perfect conditions with continuous airflow, but require 60 days or more if it is blocked by insulation or sheathing. Some materials, like oriented strand board sheathing, are particularly vulnerable to moisture damage and fail structurally at lower moisture levels than solid lumber. When professionals scan a Punta Gorda home with moisture meters, they map out problem areas and recommend drying strategies. In some cases, temporary fans and dehumidifiers targeted at specific walls accelerate the drying process.
