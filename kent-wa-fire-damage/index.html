Preventing Future Electrical Fires

Several upgrades can reduce the risk of future electrical fires in your home. Whole-home surge protection diverts power surges before they damage equipment or wiring. AFCI breakers detect arc conditions and shut down circuits before fires start. GFCI outlets prevent shock hazards near water sources. Proper circuit sizing prevents overload conditions that generate dangerous heat. Adequate outlet availability reduces reliance on extension cords and power strips. Ventilation around electrical equipment prevents heat buildup in breaker panels and appliances. Professional inspection of electrical systems every few years catches problems before they become hazards. Routine electrical maintenance should be part of your home safety planning. Upgrading outdated wiring and systems eliminates many historical fire risks. New construction standards applied during restoration make your home safer than before the fire.
